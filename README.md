# phntsmgr-v0
This is the official website for my upcoming indie game PHNTSMGR ver.0.
Link here: phntsmgr-v0.neocities.org

# Why create... this?
Instead of working on my game (like I'm supposed to), I decided that building a website for it is a cool way to introduce
people to my game and its characters. I could very well just create a social media account to do just that, but the thing
with modern social media is that the amount of creative liberty that goes in a post can and will never match the pure power of HTML, CSS and Javascript (and why not show off my coding skills while at it?)

# Features
- Music Player with a playlist of songs I think match the atmosphere/personality of each character.
- Announcement board with updates for the website/game.
- A public chatroom for fun :p
- Illustrations with a leitmotif and lyrics that sum up each MC as a whole (idk it's just cool)

# Synopsis (if anyone's interested)
Buzzing lights, the smell of damp carpet, and unease lingering in the air. Nique is unaware of how he got there, but
everything is jarringly familiar. He thought he had just escaped the hell that was his own home, but what was this?
Why is it that no matter what he does, where he runs, there was no way out? And why is it that, the stranger who has been
following him around this whole time, definitely knows more about this place than he lets on?

PHTNSMGR ver.0 an upcoming psychological horror indie game, heavily inspired by The Backrooms and liminal spaces, and aims to explore themes of insanity, apathy, the fragility of the human mind, and what happens when even friends can't trust each other. (Subject to change)
