made in godot

Godot is a completely free, open-source and absolutely goated game engine. I haven't actually tried many other game engines but Godot is absolutely the way to go. It has support for 2D, 3D games and html5, desktop and mobile. So why not?

no generative ai used

I'll be transparent -- I did use AI, but for minor debugging and image background removal only. I do not support generative AI as it harms artists like me (and many other people) in the creative industry. Also, it is just a lame excuse to skip the learning curve of making art entirely. (And the point of art is to enjoy the process of creation and express complex concepts. With gen AI, isn't that just stripping away the entire point of art?) ☃

completely free-to-play

It's also going to be open source! We all love open source :D This is because I want as much people to play my game as possible -- no trouble of pirating needed. Happiness shouldn't be locked behind a paywall (boo society). ☃
